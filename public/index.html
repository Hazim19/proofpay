ProofPay

Escrow that pays on evidence, not trust. The client funds a milestone through PayPal, the freelancer delivers, and AI verifies the work against the contract criteria before any money is released.

PayPal: … AI: …
1Describe & draftThe client describes the job; AI drafts milestones with measurable, weighted acceptance criteria.
2Fund via PayPalThe client approves in PayPal Checkout. The money is authorised and held — not sent.
3Verify & releaseAI checks the delivery criterion by criterion with cited evidence. Payment follows the verdict: earned share out, the rest refunded.
